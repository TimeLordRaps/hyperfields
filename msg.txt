Carry the commutativity/associativity claim on the mechanics-dynamics bridge

The declared architecture names a change/dynamic between admissible operation
application and labelled transition that has to do with commutativity and
associativity. That content belongs on the existing hypermechanics ->
hyperdynamics bridge, not on a new one: a second bridge with the same endpoints
would be two declarations of one correspondence, and the record's own
endpoint-distinctness guard refuses it.

What is added is grounded in the two fields' actual code rather than in the
declaration's wording:

- hypermechanics.independent(left, right) is a disjoint-footprint criterion:
  it returns true when neither rule writes a place the other writes or reads.
  That is a *sufficient* condition for two enabled rewrites to commute.
- hyperdynamics.couple_paths and coupled_product join two supplied paths under
  an explicit paired-position boundary rule, and both say in their own
  docstrings that the pairing is a caller-declared schedule rather than a
  universal clock.

So the bridge now maps the criterion to commuting labelled transitions and maps
rule application order to trajectory composition order, and it excludes the two
claims the code does not support: commutativity as a universal law, and
associativity derived from commutativity. Associativity is a property of how
three or more steps group; no clause in either field states it, so it is
recorded as an obligation rather than asserted.

The bridge stays OPEN. Nothing here promotes it.
